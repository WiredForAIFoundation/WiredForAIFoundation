Past event

Global Wired For AI Launch Event

The Next Era of AI Leadership: March 20, 2026 · 12:00–1:00 PM PT · San Francisco & online via Zoom

The launch brought product leaders together to define the playbook for AI product managers: frameworks, architectures and strategies for building the next generation of AI-native products. Featured speaker: Vince Kohli.

Agenda

  • Keynote: Wired for Vision, Mission and Values

  • The Rise of the AI-Native Product Manager

    AI PMs will prototype ideas themselves, experiment rapidly and work across design, engineering and data science. They will design multimodal experiences (voice, vision and context) and systems that extend into the physical world through robotics and intelligent devices.

  • Designing the Future: AI PMs as Architects of Industry Transformation

    From education and healthcare to finance, commerce and space, products are moving from static tools to autonomous systems that reason and decide in real time. AI PMs must design platforms for industry-level change, balancing innovation with trust and human-centred design.

  • The Human Edge in the AI Era

    When data and models are everywhere, the differentiator is judgement: clear thinking and turning information into good decisions. AI PMs must amplify human strengths such as creativity, strategy, empathy and ethical reasoning.

  • Closing remarks, Q&A, networking and next steps
