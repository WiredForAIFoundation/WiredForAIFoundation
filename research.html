Research

Open research for responsible AI

We support open, interdisciplinary research on how AI can be safe, fair, explainable and good for people.

Focus areas

Research themes

🧠

Explainable AI (XAI)

Methods and interfaces that make AI decisions understandable to non-experts.

💜

Measuring empathy

Frameworks for evaluating whether AI interactions respect and support users.

⚖️

Fairness & bias

Tools to detect and reduce bias in data and models.

🌍

AI & society

The impact of AI on work, education and communities.

🤖

Human–AI collaboration

How people and AI can work together most effectively.

🔐

Safety & trust

Building AI systems that people can rely on.
