About Wired For AI Foundation (boilerplate)

Wired For AI Foundation is a non-profit initiative that makes artificial intelligence human-centred, empathetic and accessible to everyone. Through education, open research, community programmes and independent media coverage, the foundation helps people understand, shape and benefit from AI.
