Manifesto

The AI Product Manager Manifesto

"We are not just managing products; we are directing intelligence."

Principle 1

Embrace Non-Determinism

Traditional software is deterministic: the same input yields the same output. AI is not. We must abandon the illusion of perfect control and design for probability instead. Our job is not to eliminate uncertainty, but to build experiences that stay resilient, graceful and useful even when the model's output is unexpected.

Principle 2

Evaluation is the New Specification

Writing a PRD is no longer enough. The core artifact of an AI PM is the evaluation framework. If you cannot measure it systematically, you cannot improve it. We put robust, automated and human-in-the-loop evaluation pipelines ahead of static feature requirements.

Principle 3

The Flywheel is the Moat

Models will become commoditised; data and context will not. The true value of an AI product lies in its ability to learn from its users. We architect products to capture high-signal feedback organically, creating proprietary data flywheels that compound over time.

Principle 4

Safety and Ethics are First-Class Features

Trust is the currency of AI adoption. Guardrails, bias mitigation and transparency are not compliance checkboxes to tick right before launch. They are core product features that decide whether users are willing to engage with the system.

Principle 5

From Interfaces to Agents

We are moving from tools that users operate to agents that operate on behalf of users. We must design systems that can act autonomously, and that requires new paradigms for permission, oversight and user intervention.
