About us

Our mission

AI is changing every part of life. We believe its future should be shaped by empathy, ethics and inclusion, and that everyone deserves a seat at the table.

🎯

Mission

To make artificial intelligence human-centred, empathetic and accessible. We do this by educating communities, supporting responsible innovators and advancing open research.

🔭

Vision

A world where every person can understand, shape and benefit from AI, and where AI systems are designed with empathy for the people they serve.

Our story

From AI PM collective to foundation

  • 2025: Reporting from the front lines

    Wired For AI began covering major AI and cybersecurity conferences, including HumanX 2025 and the RSAC 2025 Launch Pad.

  • March 20, 2026: Global launch

    The Wired For AI Initiative launched with The Next Era of AI Leadership, a collective that bridges cutting-edge AI research and scalable, user-centric product development for AI product managers.

  • 2026: Growing the newsroom

    Coverage expanded to HumanX 2026, RSAC 2026 and TechCrunch 2026.

  • Today: Wired For AI Foundation

    Education, community, research and independent media for human-centred AI.

Values

What we stand for

💜

Empathy first

We start with people, their needs, emotions and context, before technology.

🌍

Inclusion

AI must work for everyone, across geographies, languages, abilities and incomes.

🔍

Transparency

We champion explainable AI and open knowledge.

⚖️

Ethics & fairness

We work to identify and reduce bias and harm in AI systems.

🤝

Collaboration

We partner with universities, companies, non-profits and communities.

🌱

Impact

We measure success by the lives we improve.

Why it matters

The challenge

The AI divide

Access to AI skills and tools is unequal. Without action, AI could widen existing social and economic gaps.

The empathy gap

Many AI systems are optimised for engagement or efficiency, not for human wellbeing.

The trust gap

People need to understand how AI makes decisions before they can trust it.

Editor-in-Chief

Vince Kohli

Wired For AI is led editorially by Vince Kohli, a Silicon Valley empathy scholar and impact tech investor. He covers generative AI, agentic AI, AI leadership and emerging cybersecurity startups. He argues that the 21st century is the Age of Empathy, and that empathy is the defining skill for founders, leaders and AI.
