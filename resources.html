AI glossary

Artificial Intelligence (AI)

Computer systems that perform tasks that normally require human intelligence, such as understanding language, recognising images or making decisions.

Machine Learning (ML)

A branch of AI in which systems learn patterns from data instead of being explicitly programmed.

Large Language Model (LLM)

An AI model trained on large amounts of text that can understand and generate human language.

Explainable AI (XAI)

Techniques that make AI decisions understandable to humans.

Bias

Systematic errors in AI outputs that unfairly favour or disadvantage certain groups, often inherited from training data.

Empathic AI

AI designed to recognise and respond appropriately to human needs, emotions and context, while being honest that it is AI.

Safety

Using AI responsibly: quick tips

✅

Verify facts

AI can sound confident and still be wrong. Check important information.

🔒

Protect privacy

Don't share sensitive personal data with AI tools.

🧠

Think critically

Use AI to support your thinking, not replace it.

🏷️

Be transparent

Disclose when content is AI-generated.
