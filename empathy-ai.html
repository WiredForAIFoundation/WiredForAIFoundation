Empathy in AI

Designing AI with a human heart

Empathic AI means systems that recognise human context, respect emotions and act in people's best interests. It never means manipulating them.

Principles

Our Empathic AI principles

👂

1. Understand context

Design for the real lives, cultures and emotions of users, not average personas.

🛡️

2. Protect dignity

Never exploit emotions for engagement or profit.

🔍

3. Be explainable

People deserve to know when and how AI is shaping decisions about them.

⚖️

4. Be fair

Test for bias across groups, and fix it.

🙋

5. Keep humans in the loop

AI should augment human judgement, especially in sensitive situations.

🌱

6. Measure wellbeing

Success means people are better off, not just more engaged.

Frequently asked questions

Can AI really be empathetic?

AI does not feel emotions the way humans do. It can be designed to recognise human needs and respond respectfully and helpfully. We call this designed empathy, and it must always be honest about being AI.

Isn't emotional AI risky?

Yes, if it is misused. That is why our principles put dignity, transparency and human oversight first.
