Programmes

Programmes that change lives

From classrooms to startups, our programmes help people learn, build and lead with AI.

🎓

AI Literacy for All

Free, beginner-friendly workshops that explain what AI is, how it works and how to use it safely, for students, parents, seniors and non-technical professionals.

🧒

Young AI Innovators

Hands-on clubs and camps where school and university students build real AI projects focused on their communities.

👩‍🏫

Educators' AI Toolkit

Training and ready-to-use lesson plans that help teachers bring AI into the classroom responsibly.

🚀

Founders for Good

Mentorship, office hours and pitch practice for founders building impact-driven AI startups, guided by experienced Silicon Valley mentors.

👩‍💻

Women & Underrepresented Groups in AI

Scholarships, mentoring circles and career pathways that widen participation in AI.

🏛️

AI for Non-profits

Pro-bono support that helps charities and NGOs adopt AI to amplify their impact.

How it works

Our approach

  • Listen

    We co-design every programme with the community it serves.

  • Learn

    Practical, hands-on learning, free or low-cost, in person and online.

  • Build

    Participants build real projects that solve real problems.

  • Share

    All curricula and tools are published openly so others can reuse them.
